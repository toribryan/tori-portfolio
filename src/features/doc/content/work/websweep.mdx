Exam content leaks to homework-help sites, forums and file-shares, and the
institution is usually the last to know. WebSweep scans the open web for
leaked assessment content, shows what has already escaped, and turns a find
into an action: a takedown notice, or swapping the affected questions so the
exam stays usable. It ships as one half of Vault by Proctorio, alongside
ScreenBlock.

The product design, the flows and the product decisions were Tory Barden's.

## A branded style of the new system

WebSweep was the first product to ship on the
[overhauled design system](/work/design-system-overhaul). It was first
designed on the legacy system, and when the overhaul shipped in January 2026
it moved onto the new one before launch. It uses a branded style of the
system: the same components and semantic tokens as everything else, styled
for the product.

The first tier launched to customers in May 2026, after an April beta.
