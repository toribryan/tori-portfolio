## The audit

I audited the site's core templates, including the homepage, article pages,
vendor listings, and gallery experiences, and found three problems working
against the brand:

- Navigation complexity. Content categories overlapped and had no hierarchy,
  so a reader had a hard time moving between inspiration, vendor discovery, and
  editorial content.
- Visual inconsistency. Typography, spacing, and imagery were dated and didn't
  hang together, which weakens a brand in an industry that sells on looks.
- Ad intrusiveness. Oversized ad placements interrupted articles and made them
  hard to read through.

## A clearer information architecture

I restructured the taxonomy around clearer top-level categories: Wedding Pros
& Venues, Fashion & Beauty, Weddings, Events, and Shop, with Podcasts, Sign Up,
and Log In surfaced separately from the content categories. The goal was a
primary nav a reader can hold in their head, with clear paths between editorial
and vendor content, so someone who came for inspiration can get to a vendor
without hunting for the way there.

## Where it stands

The project is still in development, so there are no numbers yet. What the new
structure is built to do: give the sales side more ad placements to offer, make
content easier to find once categories stop overlapping, and give the brand a
more consistent face in a crowded bridal market.
