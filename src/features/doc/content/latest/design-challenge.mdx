## The prompt

45 minutes. The core design element: Balance. Take one idea and build two versions of it, one symmetric, one asymmetric.

## My approach

I designed a magazine page, Mocha, and let its layout answer the prompt.

## Two aesthetic directions

I ran the pair twice. Once in a clean editorial treatment on solid blue. Once with organic blob shapes surrounding a lighter card. Each treatment makes its own case for what balance looks like on the page.

## What I learned

Symmetry felt safer and more editorial. Asymmetry gave the layout tension, which suited a prompt about balance.
